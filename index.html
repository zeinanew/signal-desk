Add, remove or turn off a source

  1. Open sources.json in your repository.
  2. To turn a source off, change "enabled": true to false. To remove it, delete its block.
  3. To add one, copy an existing block and change the id, name, url and feed. Most sites have a feed at /feed or /rss.xml.
  4. Commit the change. The site refreshes with it straight away.
